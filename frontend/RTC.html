How the timing model works

Castle Garrison is the current castle holder and is not counted as a hit. Observed Timer uses Gather Left plus Enemy March. Relative to Friendly Hit anchors an enemy rally to Infantry killer, Softener, or Castle holder with a chosen offset.

Friendly launch point = desired hit point - rally gathering duration - leader march time. Friendly launch points are sorted, then shown as LAUNCH FIRST and relative delays after the previous friendly launcher.

Friendly State means: Ready = waiting for your declaration; Gathering = rally is already open and Remaining is fill time left; Marching = rally has launched and Remaining is march time left.

Attack order is Infantry killer, optional Softener, Castle holder, observed enemy counters, Alliance B +1 second after Enemy Final Counter, then Post-wave reinforce.
