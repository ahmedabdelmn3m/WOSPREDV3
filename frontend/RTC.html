How the timing model works

Enemy countdowns share one snapshot. A 22 second enemy rally hits before a 24 second enemy rally, even if it was entered second.

Friendly launch point = desired hit point - rally gathering duration - leader march time. Friendly launch points are sorted, then shown as LAUNCH FIRST and relative delays after the previous friendly launcher.

Live mode checks whether a not-launched, gathering, or marching rally can still hit the desired point. Impossible timing is shown as a warning instead of being hidden.
