How the timing model works

Castle Garrison is the current holder and is not counted as an incoming hit. Gather + March calculates a landing from the shared snapshot. Relative to Friendly Rally uses the selected friendly leader's planned hit plus a signed offset.

Friendly launch point = desired hit point - rally gathering duration - leader march time. Friendly launch points are sorted, then shown as LAUNCH FIRST and relative delays after the previous friendly launcher.

Friendly State means: Ready = waiting for your declaration; Gathering = rally is already open and Remaining is fill time left; Marching = rally has launched and Remaining is march time left.

Every enabled Alliance A leader becomes part of the capture wave; known roles guide the order and Castle holder stays last when available. Alliance B leaders targeted by later enemies are placed where needed, then every remaining enabled Alliance B rally lands after the final enemy counter, one second apart.

Hero joiner damage uses Attack x (1 + Attack bonuses) x Lethality x (1 + Lethality bonuses) / 100, then applies Damage Up and Attack Damage Up. Defense mirrors it with Defense and Health. Only the primary skill from the first four modeled joiners contributes.
